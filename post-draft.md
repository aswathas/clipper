Every day we save things. A link. A screenshot. A paragraph. A "I'll read this later" that never gets read.

They live in browser bookmarks, a Screenshots folder, three different note apps, a Notion that has 47 abandoned pages. None of them talk to each other. None of them remember you.

I have been quietly building a small thing for this.

A local-first second brain. It captures what you save — any URL, any image, any text — and turns it into something you can actually think with later. No cloud. No subscription. No infinite feed. Just a calm little library that lives on your machine and gets smarter the more you use it.

This is the dashboard. It is the first thing you see when you open it.
